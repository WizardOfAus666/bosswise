# BossWise

**BossWise** is an Old School RuneScape bossing and PvM companion plugin for RuneLite. It evaluates which bosses and Slayer monsters your account is ready for, builds optimized gear and supply loadouts for any GP budget, and provides quick strategy guides right in the RuneLite sidebar.

## Features

### 1. Real-Time Account & Quest Sync
- Automatically reads your logged-in character's stats, combat level, completed quests, equipped gear, and bank items.
- Distinguishes base skill levels from temporary boosts so unlock checks stay accurate.

### 2. Boss & Monster Readiness (75+ Encounters)
Categorizes bosses, raids, Slayer creatures, and Wilderness encounters based on your current stats and quests:
- **READY (Green):** You meet all required quests and skill levels.
- **ALMOST READY (Amber):** Close to unlocking — shows the exact missing levels or quests.
- **LOCKED (Red):** Missing major quest lines or skill milestones.
- Filter by status, category (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Endgame, Slayer, Raids, Wilderness), difficulty, or solo/group, and sort by recommendation score, GP/hr, or gear cost.

### 3. Gear Budget Optimizer & Upgrade Advisor
- Enter any custom GP budget (or pick quick presets) to generate **Budget**, **Best Value**, **High Tier**, and **Max BiS** setups tailored to your stats and quests.
- Calculates real DPS using OSRS Wiki item and monster stats, accounting for Salve amulet, Dragonbane, Demonbane, Kalphitebane, Ratbane, Void, Wilderness weapons, and mandatory protections (dragonfire shields, mirror/V's shield, boots of stone, leaf-bladed weapons, spears).
- **Upgrade Advisor:** Highlights the highest-impact slot upgrades for your current gear using live Grand Exchange prices.

### 4. DPS Lab & Setup Comparison
- Test gear swaps, prayers, potions, and Slayer task bonuses against any monster variant and compare setups side-by-side.

### 5. Inventory & Supply Builder
- Generates food, potion, rune, ammo, and utility inventories with live Grand Exchange trip cost estimates.

### 6. Strategy Guides & Progression Roadmap
- Step-by-step fight strategies, location & travel directions, recommended overhead and offensive prayers, phase warnings, notable drops, and pre-fight checklists.
- Tracks actionable skill, quest, and gear milestones to unlock your next boss tier.

## How to Use

1. Enable **BossWise** in your RuneLite plugin list and click the **golden scale & skull** icon on the right sidebar.
2. Log in to Old School RuneScape to scan your stats and quests automatically.
3. Browse **Boss Finder** to pick a target, or open **Gear Optimizer**, **DPS Lab**, **Guides**, or **Supplies** directly from the tabs.
