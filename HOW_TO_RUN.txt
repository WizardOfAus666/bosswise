  BOSSWISE - Universal OSRS Monster Combat Intelligence & Strategy Engine

HOW TO RUN (READY-TO-RUN LAUNCHERS):
------------------------------------
1. Extract this entire zip folder anywhere on your PC (e.g. Desktop).
2. Choose ANY of the easy launcher scripts:
   - Double-click "run-plugin.bat" or "START_BOSSWISE.bat"
   - Or in PowerShell run: .\run-plugin.ps1  (or .\run-bosswise.ps1)
3. RuneLite will launch with BossWise pre-installed on the right sidebar!
4. Click the golden scale with skull icon on the RuneLite sidebar.
5. Log into Old School RuneScape: your character's skills, combat level,
   bank gear, and completed quests automatically sync in real time!

1. 100% Monster Strategy Coverage:

MASSIVE GEAR DATABASE & COMBAT INTELLIGENCE EXPANSION:
------------------------------------------------------
1. 242+ Verified OSRS Equipment Items:
   - Structured JSON database (`equipment.json`) with attack/defence stats,
     melee strength, ranged strength, magic damage %, prayer bonuses,
     attack speeds, requirements, tradeability, and special attacks.

2. OSRS Wiki MediaWiki Integration & Caching:
   - Asynchronous, rate-limited MediaWiki querying with polite user-agent.
   - Offline fallback to bundled verified baselines (`monsters.json` & `equipment.json`).
   - Disk and memory caching with TTL and data confidence tracking.

3. Special Equipment Effects & Stacking Catalog:
   - Dragonbane: Dragon hunter lance (+20% acc/dmg), Dragon hunter crossbow (+30% acc/dmg).
   - Demonbane: Arclight (+70% acc, +30% dmg), Emberlight (+70% acc, +36% dmg), Scorching bow.
   - Undead: Salve amulet (+15%), Salve (e)/(ei) (+20%).
   - Stacking Rules: Salve takes priority over Slayer helm (no double stack). Void stacks with Salve!
   - Kalphitebane: Keris Partisan piercing and 3x special proc (+37.2% expected damage).
   - Ratbane: Bone mace / shortbow / staff (+10 flat max hit).
   - Wilderness Weapons: +50% accuracy and +50% damage in Wilderness.
   - Tumeken's Shadow: 3x magic attack and magic damage multiplier.

4. Multi-Setup Generation Engine:
   - Generates ALL viable setups across styles (Melee, Ranged, Magic, Specialised)
     and tiers (Budget, Best Value, High Tier, Max BiS, Specialised).
   - Filters out non-viable styles (e.g. Melee against Kree'arra, non-spear against Corp,
     Melee/Ranged against Kraken).

5. Side-by-Side 5-Setup Comparison:
   - Click "[Compare Setups (5)]" to inspect up to 5 complete setups side-by-side.
   - Compares DPS, Hit Chance %, Max Hit, Total Loadout Cost, Prayer Bonus,
     Average Defence, and DPS Efficiency (DPS per Million GP).
   - Slot Matrix displaying side-by-side gear choices across all 11 slots.
   - Matched 28-slot inventory setups for each tier.

6. Account Requirement Validation:
   - Validates character Attack, Strength, Defence, Ranged, Magic, Prayer, Slayer,
     and quest completions.
   - Highlights missing requirements clearly on equipped items with warning alerts.

7. Zero Text Clipping Guaranteed:
   - Calibrated for standard 225px and 242px RuneLite sidebar widths using `WrapText`.
